I am a PhD student at the C.N Yang Institute for Theoretical Physics and the Department of Physics and Astronomy at Stony Brook University in New York. I am primarily interested in the intersection of cosmology and particle physics. I am working on new applications of the effective field theory of large scale structure, partuclarly focusing on lensing observables to line up potentail cross correlations. I am also interesting in pushing the EFT (or other perturbative/analytic models) to new physics of dark matter and dark energy to probe the fundamental consituents of our world. In the past, I have worked on machine learning cosmological emulators (masters degree) and taggers in the ATLAS detector (undergraduate degree), and done an advanced special topics study on classical Yang-Mills theory in the language of differential geometry and group theory during. 

In my free time I like to hike trails, climb rocks, and play guitars.
